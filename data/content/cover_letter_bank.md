# ── SECTION 1: Para 2 Style Reference — by Domain ─────────────
# How to structure the challenge → action → result narrative.
# Each entry shows the tone and flow — NOT text to copy.

## [YOUR_DOMAIN_1] — e.g. Product Analytics

# Style: Lead with the business challenge, then your specific analytical intervention,
# then the measurable outcome. Avoid generic "I worked on X" framing.
# Reference the JD's specific product area or metric when synthesizing.
#
# Example tone (replace with your own content):
# "At [Company A], the core challenge was [specific problem]. I designed and shipped
#  [specific solution — with methodology], which [metric outcome]. This directly enabled
#  [business impact that mattered to the team]."


## [YOUR_DOMAIN_2] — e.g. Commercial / Pricing

# Style: Lead with the commercial context (margin pressure, efficiency gap, pricing
# complexity), then the analytical approach, then the quantified outcome.
#
# Example tone:
# "In my role at [Company B], I tackled [specific commercial challenge]. By building
#  [specific model or framework], we achieved [metric] — translating directly into
#  [revenue / cost / efficiency outcome]."


# ── SECTION 2: Para 3 Thematic Frames ────────────────────────
# Complementary themes to pair with the Para 2 primary story.
# Choose the theme with the strongest JD keyword overlap.

## Leadership / Team Capability Building
# For JDs mentioning: team leadership, people management, mentoring, capability building.
# Frame as: what you built, how you structured the team, what they now deliver.

## Experimentation / Measurement
# For JDs mentioning: A/B testing, experimentation, incrementality, attribution.
# Frame as: the measurement challenge, your methodological approach, business adoption.

## Data Platform / Infrastructure
# For JDs mentioning: data infrastructure, tooling, data quality, platform ownership.
# Frame as: the system before, what you built or improved, the delivery impact.

## Strategic Influence / Stakeholder Impact
# For JDs mentioning: cross-functional collaboration, exec reporting, data literacy.
# Frame as: a specific decision your analysis changed or a capability gap you addressed.
